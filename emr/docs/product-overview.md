# ConcertoCare EMR — Product Overview

## The Problem We Solve

Home care organizations run on callbacks, paper faxes, and reactive care. Clinicians spend more time charting than caring. Care gaps go unnoticed until a patient ends up in the ED. Territories are managed in spreadsheets. Documents pile up unread in inboxes.

**ConcertoCare EMR was built to change that** — an AI-native platform purpose-built for in-home care that automates the administrative burden, surfaces risks before they become crises, and keeps clinicians focused on patients, not paperwork.

---

## Platform Modules

### 1. AI-Powered Visit Documentation
Clinicians dictate or type a free-form note after a visit. The AI automatically produces:
- A complete **SOAP note** (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan)
- **Extracted vitals** parsed from natural language
- **Action items** with priorities and due dates
- **Workflow triggers** for follow-up care

Zero structured data entry required. The clinician talks; the chart writes itself.

---

### 2. Pre-Visit AI Brief
Before every visit, the AI reviews the patient's full record and delivers a concise brief to the clinician covering:
- **Priority focus areas** for today's visit
- **Clinical alerts** (abnormal labs, missed meds, care gaps)
- **Medication review flags**
- **Risk summary** and recent changes
- **Conversation starters** tailored to the patient

Think of it as a 60-second attending-level huddle before every home visit.

---

### 3. AI Risk Stratification
Every patient receives a continuously updated **hospitalization and ED risk score** powered by Claude's clinical reasoning. The dashboard surfaces high-risk patients so care coordinators can intervene proactively — before a 911 call.

Risk tiers:
- 🔴 High (≥70%) — immediate outreach triggered automatically
- 🟡 Moderate (40–69%) — weekly check-in scheduled
- 🟢 Low (<40%) — standard care cadence

---

### 4. Automated Workflow Builder
A drag-and-drop automation engine — think Power Automate built for home care. Clinical staff build workflows visually, no code required.

**Example workflows already included:**
- Post-visit follow-up: 24h after a completed visit → send SMS check-in → create task if no response
- Risk escalation: when AI risk score crosses 70% → notify care coordinator → schedule urgent visit
- Lab result received: fax arrives with critical value → alert physician → create urgent task
- Discharge follow-up: patient discharged from hospital → schedule welcome-home visit within 48h

Every action in the EMR can trigger an automated workflow. No manual triage, no dropped balls.

---

### 5. Fax & Document Intelligence
Faxes arrive and are automatically:
1. **OCR'd** — raw text extracted from the image
2. **Classified** — lab result, discharge summary, referral, order, insurance doc, etc.
3. **Data extracted** — diagnoses (ICD-10), medications (RxNorm), lab values, patient identifiers
4. **Patient-matched** — linked to the right chart using MRN, insurance ID, or name + DOB
5. **Workflow-triggered** — relevant automations fire immediately

Powered by AWS Comprehend Medical for clinical NLP. Staff only touch documents that genuinely need human review.

---

### 6. Engagement & Outreach
AI-personalized outreach across SMS, phone, and email — at scale.

- **Appointment reminders** — personalized to the patient's care context
- **Medication adherence check-ins** — proactive, not reactive
- **Care gap closures** — annual wellness, missing follow-ups
- **Discharge follow-ups** — auto-scheduled within care protocols
- **Bulk campaigns** — target any patient cohort in seconds

Every message is generated by Claude based on the patient's record, not a generic template.

---

### 7. Schedule & Territory Management
A **time-grid calendar** showing every clinician's day — visit blocks color-coded by type (SN, PT, OT, SW, HHA), with live/completed status, risk scores, and AI brief indicators.

**Territory admin** maps zip codes to providers:
- See every patient in a zip code with one click
- Drag-select zip codes and assign to a provider
- Visual territory overview grouped by clinician

---

### 8. Admin & Access Control
- **Role-based access**: Admin, Physician, Nurse, Therapist, Social Worker, Aide, Care Coordinator, Billing
- **User management**: Create, edit, deactivate, password reset
- **NPI tracking** for every clinician
- **Audit trail** via structured logging on every AI action

---

### 9. Integration-Ready API
Direct API access for external tools:
- **Write progress notes directly** — mobile apps, dictation services, external EHRs
- **FHIR R4 compatible** — native read/write to AWS HealthLake
- **Webhook-ready** — Twilio for fax intake, SMS delivery

## The AI Difference

Most EMRs bolt AI on as an afterthought. ConcertoCare was designed AI-first.

| Capability | Traditional EMR | ConcertoCare |
|-----------|----------------|--------------|
| Visit notes | Clinician types structured fields | Dictate freely → SOAP generated automatically |
| Risk flagging | Manual review of labs | Continuous AI scoring on every patient |
| Fax handling | Staff reads and types | Auto-classified, extracted, and routed |
| Care gaps | Reports run weekly | Surfaced before every visit |
| Follow-up workflows | Manual task assignment | Triggered automatically by clinical events |
| Outreach messages | Generic templates | AI-personalized per patient |

The AI uses a **clinical reasoning agent loop** — it doesn't just run a single prompt. It queries the patient record iteratively, like a clinician reviewing a chart, then synthesizes a response. This is the same pattern used in MedAgentBench, a Stanford benchmark for medical AI.

## Privacy & Compliance

Built for HIPAA from the ground up:

- **AWS Bedrock** — Claude runs inside your AWS account under a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement. Patient data never leaves your cloud.
- **AWS HealthLake** — FHIR R4-compliant storage, HIPAA-eligible
- **AWS Comprehend Medical** — clinical NLP under BAA
- **Bedrock Guardrails** — optional PHI masking on all AI inputs/outputs
- **No PHI sent to third parties** — the AI runs entirely within AWS infrastructure

## Who This Is For

ConcertoCare EMR is designed for **home care organizations** — agencies providing skilled nursing, physical therapy, occupational therapy, social work, and personal care in patients' homes.

It is especially well-suited for organizations that:
- Serve **complex, high-risk patients** (post-discharge, chronic disease, elderly)
- Want to **reduce avoidable hospitalizations** through proactive risk management
- Are frustrated by **administrative burden** consuming clinical time
- Need better **care coordination** across multidisciplinary teams
- Are exploring AI but need it to be **HIPAA-compliant and trustworthy**
